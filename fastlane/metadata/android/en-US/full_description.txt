A tiny, fast tap counter built for AMOLED screens. No ads, no tracking, no network access.

<b>Counting</b>
<ul>
<li>Tap anywhere to count up, with a short vibration as feedback</li>
<li>Hold anywhere to switch between the control panel and the counting view</li>
<li>Counting views: pure black (screen looks off while you count), dots, or tally marks</li>
<li>Dots and tally fill the screen, buzz when it is full and keep counting in the next colour, overwriting the oldest marks</li>
<li>Slow pixel shifting to protect AMOLED screens from burn-in</li>
<li>-1 button and hold-to-reset on the panel</li>
</ul>

<b>Settings</b>
<ul>
<li>Font size, dot/tally size, colours and how often they change</li>
<li>Optional maximum count</li>
<li>Custom vibration patterns for every event, written as plain milliseconds like "40 60 40"</li>
<li>Keep the screen on while counting</li>
</ul>

The count is saved between launches. Only the vibration permission is used.
