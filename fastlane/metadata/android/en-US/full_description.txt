A tiny, fast tap counter built for AMOLED screens. No ads, no tracking. The only network request is the update check you start yourself in the settings.

<b>Counting</b>
<ul>
<li>Tap anywhere to count up, with a short vibration as feedback</li>
<li>Hold anywhere to switch between the control panel and the counting view</li>
<li>Counting views: pure black (screen looks off while you count), dots, tally marks, or a self-playing snake game</li>
<li>Dots and tally fill the screen, then drain one mark per tap until the next lap, which changes the colour, buzzes and starts over</li>
<li>Slow pixel shifting to protect AMOLED screens from burn-in</li>
<li>-1 and adjust buttons on the panel: set the count and the target, reset, undo</li>
<li>Optional counting with the volume buttons, or with a floating bubble over other apps</li>
<li>Dhikr mode: import text profiles (any language, one file each) and count their slides one after another; swipe to skip a slide or switch between Arabic, transliteration and meaning; use Goto in the menu to search and jump to a slide; share .dhikr files from other apps to import them; completed screen with confetti</li>
</ul>

<b>Settings</b>
<ul>
<li>Font size, dot/tally size and lap length, six colour themes with dark and light mode</li>
<li>Optional "count reached" target that buzzes once; counting continues past it</li>
<li>Custom vibration patterns for every event, written as plain milliseconds like "40 60 40"</li>
<li>Keep the screen on while counting</li>
<li>Optional Do Not Disturb while the app is open (priority only or alarms only), restored when you leave</li>
</ul>

The count is saved between launches. Permissions: vibration, internet (only for that update check), and Do Not Disturb access only if you turn that option on.
